#+title: Changelog

* 0.1.1

- Highlight the Vertico posframe child frame with the meeting accent border
  and focus flash.
- Frame Transient menus with the accent on the header and mode lines, and
  follow the popup even when another window remains selected.
- Add =meeting-border-width= for the accent fringe width.
- Add =meeting-blink-count= and =meeting-blink-interval= to control the
  focus blink, and make =meeting-flash-duration= the total blink time when
  the interval is derived.

* 0.1.0

- Initial public release.
- Flash the newly focused window and keep a theme-derived accent on its fringe,
  mode line, header line, and tab line.
- Follow window, buffer, minibuffer, frame, and optional Transient focus changes.
- Add customization options for the flash duration, flash strength, and accent
  color.
- Add standalone ERT coverage for enable/disable cleanup, accent regeneration,
  focus tracking, flash timers, refresh scheduling, and optional Transient use.
